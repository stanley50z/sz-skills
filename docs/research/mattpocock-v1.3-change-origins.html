Preserving the rule is different from preserving the spin. I removed repeated safeguards from implement and put them behind TDD pointers; shortened the TDD wording substantially; changed prototype presentation; changed review timing/ownership; and softened writing rules. Those changes can affect an agent's emphasis even when the requirements still appear somewhere. Passing tests does not prove identical model behavior.

Given your preference, I would treat your preexisting custom forks as the baseline to preserve, port only release compatibility deliberately, and review the optional rewrites separately. The TDD/implement restructuring and prototype rewrite deserve the closest review before you decide whether to keep them.
